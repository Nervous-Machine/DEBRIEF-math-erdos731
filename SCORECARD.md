# The claimed solution of Erdős Problem #731 (the least non-divisor of the central binomial coefficient)

Erdős, Graham, Ruzsa and Straus asked for a reasonable function f(n) such that, for almost all n, the least m not dividing the central binomial coefficient is asymptotic to f(n). In June 2026 Eric Li posted a paper, and in July a Lean development, resolving the problem under an explicit dyadic-regularity reading of 'reasonable': a best deterministic scale exists for tightness in density, and no dyadically regular f gives the asymptotic equivalence asked for. Whether that reading answers the problem as posed is a judgment a number theorist has to make. Everything here is open.
